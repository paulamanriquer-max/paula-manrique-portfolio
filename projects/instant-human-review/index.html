Instant Human Review

Design Challenge

01

High-stakes communication needed more trust

In healthcare, legal, education, and public service contexts, translation quality can directly affect someone’s ability to understand critical information.

02

Speed could not come at the cost of oversight

The product needed to support faster translation review while preserving the confidence expected in regulated communication.

03

The experience had to work across teams

My role was to help shape a clear product experience across user needs, operational realities, and engineering constraints.

What I Can Share Publicly

01

Led product framing

I helped translate an emerging opportunity into a product direction that balanced business value, user trust, compliance sensitivity, and technical feasibility.

02

Designed for human confidence

The design work centered on making AI support the workflow without asking users to blindly trust automation in moments where accuracy and accountability matter.

03

Prepared the experience for delivery

I created production-ready UX, UI, interaction specs, edge cases, and cross-functional documentation to help teams move from concept to implementation.

Why This Case Study Is Redacted

Design Leadership

My contribution was helping the team move from a promising idea to a credible product direction. That included clarifying the user problem, mapping the service implications, shaping the interface strategy, and aligning product, design, engineering, and operational partners around a responsible path forward.

The work required product thinking beyond screens: where AI should create speed, where human judgment should remain visible, and how to make a complex service feel understandable to the people using it.

Current Status

This case study is intentionally redacted while the product and IP process continue. A fuller version exists for future use once the right approvals are in place.

Reflection

The strongest lesson was not just how to design an AI product. It was how to design the relationship between automation, human judgment, and trust in moments where communication has real consequences.

The best AI experiences do not remove humans from the workflow. They amplify human expertise where it matters most.
